AI deployment sprint: automation discovery for a UK manufacturer

Leading a team through discovery and options appraisal to remove repetitive design work, with people kept in control.

Context

An eight-week AI Deployment Sprint delivered by Manchester Digital with Manchester Metropolitan University. I lead a team of three working with a UK manufacturer of leisure safety products.

The problem

Preparing bespoke products for automated cutting involves repetitive manual design work across several software tools that are not connected to one another.

My role

As Team Lead, I coordinate the team’s delivery while contributing directly to the technical analysis and solution design. My responsibilities span team leadership, stakeholder communication and documentation, alongside active participation in process mapping, root cause analysis, evaluating AI approaches and shaping the solution with the team.

Approach

  • Current-state process mapping, BPMN and data flow modelling.
  • Root cause analysis of where time and errors enter the process.
  • Requirements and acceptance criteria agreed with the process owner.
  • An options appraisal comparing build and buy routes against cost, risk and value.
  • Human review before anything goes to production, and careful data governance in the use of AI tools.

Outcome

The sprint is under way. Outcomes will be published here once it concludes and the client has approved what can be shared.
