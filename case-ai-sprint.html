AI Deployment Sprint: from discovery to a working MVP for a UK manufacturer

Leading discovery, business analysis and the build of a rules-based MVP that removes repetitive design work, with people kept in control at every stage.

Role
Team Lead
Period
October 2026 to present
Focus
Business analysis, solution design and build
Status
Selected by the client

Context

An eight-week AI Deployment Sprint, funded by Innovate UK and delivered by Manchester Digital with Manchester Metropolitan University. I led a team working with a UK manufacturer to reduce repetitive manual design work. Details of the client’s products and processes are kept confidential at the client’s request.

My role

As Team Lead, I was responsible for planning, risks, decisions, documentation and client communication. The work also involved much of the analysis and solution design, which I led.

How I approached it

  1. DiscoverAgree the problem and scope with the client.
  2. MapModel and time the current process in BPMN.
  3. AnalyseStudy design and production files in Python.
  4. ValidateConfirm the design rules with the client.
  5. Specify and buildWrite the specification, then build in small stories.
  6. Test and hand overCheck against approved files and document.

Discovery and analysis

  • Mapped and timed the current process in BPMN, validated with the process owner, to find where the manual effort was concentrated.
  • Analysed 130 CAD and production files in Python (ezdxf, pypdf) and documented over 40 design and manufacturing rules, validated with the client through automated reconciliation checks.
  • Conducted the business analysis: root cause analysis (fishbone and 5 Whys), MoSCoW-prioritised requirements, acceptance criteria and an options appraisal.

Building the MVP

I designed and built a rules-based MVP in Python, Flask and SQLite, with a layered architecture and human approval at each stage. I wrote the specification first, then delivered the work as small user stories, directing Claude Code against the specification. I approved each plan before any code was written and reviewed every change before it was merged.

  • Automated quality gates on every change: ruff, strict mypy, import-linter and pytest, with a separate branch for each story and all automated tests passing.
  • No AI inside the tool: the rules are applied in plain code, so the same input always gives the same result.

Outcome

The MVP cut standard order preparation by over 99%, from about 25 minutes to 12 seconds, with outputs matching the client’s approved files and loading correctly in their production software. After trialling it, the client selected it as its chosen solution.

Handover

I produced user, maintenance and troubleshooting guides to support handover to the client.
