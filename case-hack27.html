Supply Wise Engine: machine learning decision support for Rolls-Royce

Leading a six-person team at Project:Hack27 to design a tool that scores supplier forecast confidence and prescribes corrective actions.

Role
Team Lead
Period
April 2026
Focus
Data, machine learning and solution design
Status
Completed

Context

Project:Hack27 is the UK’s largest project data hackathon, hosted by Projecting Success in collaboration with Rolls-Royce, Gleeds and the Major Projects Association, at the Rolls-Royce Learning and Development Centre in Derby. I led the six-person Supply Wise team.

The challenge

Rolls-Royce supply chain forecasts were consistently overestimating actual spend, fading with every revision and offering no guidance on what to do about it. Our analysis identified a £2.8 billion gap between forecasted and actual spend. The challenge was to restore confidence and turn reactive reporting into proactive decision support.

What we built

The Supply Wise Engine, a machine learning decision-support tool that scores each supplier’s forecast confidence, identifies the behaviours driving forecast fade, and prescribes corrective actions before failure occurs.

My contribution

  • Led the team, bringing together data analytics, business intelligence, AI and machine learning, UI and UX, and product management.
  • Analysed 5,980 forecast records across 5 programmes and 50 suppliers.
  • Contributed to a Random Forest model in Python and scikit-learn, which achieved 82.6% accuracy across 19 features.
  • Collaborated on product discovery and solution design in Miro, producing a value proposition canvas, user personas, story mapping and a product vision.

Outcome

Improved forecast confidence scoring identified £30.5 million in trapped capital that could be proactively reallocated. We delivered the final presentation under real-time pressure.

Key figures

5,980 forecast records, 82.6% model accuracy, £30.5 million in trapped capital identified

Tools

Python, scikit-learn, Power BI, Miro, PowerPoint, Canva, MindView, Clipchamp
