Supply Wise Engine: machine learning decision support for Rolls-Royce

Leading the Supply Wise team at Project:Hack27 to design a tool that scores supplier forecast confidence and recommends corrective actions.

Role
Team Lead
Period
April 2026
Focus
Data, machine learning and solution design
Status
Completed

Context

Project:Hack27 is the UK’s largest project data hackathon, hosted by Projecting Success in collaboration with Rolls-Royce, Gleeds and the Major Projects Association, at the Rolls-Royce Learning and Development Centre in Derby. I led the Supply Wise team.

The challenge

The challenge focused on forecast fade in supply chain spend: forecasts that overestimate actual spend, fade with every revision and give no guidance on what to do about it. The challenge dataset covered 5,980 forecast records across 5 programmes and 50 suppliers, with a £2.8 billion gap between forecast and actual spend and an average forecast error of 26.6%.

What we built

The Supply Wise Engine, a decision-support tool that scores each supplier’s forecast confidence, identifies the behaviours driving forecast fade, and recommends corrective actions before failure occurs.

My contribution

  • Led the team, bringing together data analytics, business intelligence, machine learning, UI and UX, and product management.
  • Led product discovery in Miro, using personas, a value proposition canvas, story mapping and a product vision to agree what decision-makers needed.
  • Contributed to a Random Forest model in Python and scikit-learn that explained 82.6% of the variation in forecast error (R² 0.826) across 19 features, showing that the commitment gap and the number of revisions were the main drivers.
  • Helped shape the solution into a confidence dashboard and an action console, presented to the judges with a live demo.
