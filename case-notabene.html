Projects / Nota Bene Consulting

Using drones in transport planning: a technology and compliance assessment

A five-day consultancy project for a Sheffield transport planning consultancy, covering survey workflow, UK regulation, data privacy and software.

Role
Consultancy Intern
Period
April 2026
Focus
Technology and compliance assessment
Status
Completed

Context

Through the University of Sheffield Spring Internship Experience 2026, our team worked as consultants for Nota Bene Consulting, a Sheffield transport planning consultancy, on how it could use its DJI Mini 4 Pro drone in its transport planning work. Within five days, we produced a written report and presented our recommendations to the client in person.

My contribution

  • Co-wrote the section on drone survey workflow and data processing, from flight planning and programming to organising imagery and processing it into orthomosaics, 3D site models and visual outputs.
  • Co-wrote the software and processing requirements section, comparing photogrammetry, flight planning and anonymisation tools on compatibility, cost and limitations, and recommending a phased approach.
  • Worked with the team to make sure our recommendations met UK aviation rules and GDPR, which the client had set as a priority, and to deliver the report and presentation within the deadline.

What I took from it

A first-hand view of how consultancy work is planned and delivered, and how quickly a team from different courses can produce work a client can act on.
