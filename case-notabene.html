Projects / Nota Bene Consulting

Software assessment for a transport planning consultancy

Researching and assessing software for a real client, covering technology, data privacy compliance and cost-effectiveness, within five days.

Role
Consultancy Intern
Period
April 2026
Focus

Status
Completed

Context

The Spring Internship Experience 2026, organised by the University of Sheffield Careers and Employability Service, placed me with Nota Bene Consulting, a Sheffield-based transport planning consultancy, working alongside students from a range of courses.

What I did

  • Carried out independent research and developed a technical assessment of software for the consultancy, evaluating processing technologies, compliance with data privacy laws and cost-effectiveness.
  • Formulated recommendations ready for presentation to the client within a five-day timeframe.
  • Worked with an interdisciplinary group to develop a consultancy report for a real client, drawing on analysis, clear writing and time management.

What I took from it

A first-hand view of how professional consultancy work is planned and delivered, and how quickly a diverse team can produce work a client can act on.
