How I work

Business first, technology second. Sometimes the right answer is a small change rather than a new system.

  1. Understand

    Clarify the business goal, who is affected and what cannot change.

  2. Map

    Document how the work happens today, not how it is assumed to happen.

  3. Diagnose

    Find root causes using evidence and data, not opinion.

  4. Appraise

    Compare build, buy and simple fixes against cost, risk and value.

  5. Design

    Define requirements, integrations and acceptance criteria.

  6. Deliver and measure

    Deliver in iterations and check the change did its job.
