About me

I am a business and technology professional based in Manchester. My career began in Lisbon, in private security and airside operations, before I moved to the UK, where I supervised international repack projects, completed two degrees and built my own product. I speak Portuguese and English, and in my free time I enjoy reading, travelling and learning new skills.

My journey

My path has not been a straight line, and every step taught me something I still use.

I started working at 16, at McDonald’s, while still at school, studying during the day and working evening shifts. It taught me discipline and how to keep going when things are hard. Warehouse work as a picker and packer followed, in fast-paced and physically demanding environments.

In 2012, I trained as a private security officer and went on to work with Prosegur and Esegur, where I learned to stay alert, assertive and professional in a male-dominated field. From 2013, I also trained as a volunteer firefighter with the Bombeiros Voluntários da Póvoa de Santa Iria, completing courses in ambulance crew skills, life support, vehicle rescue and firefighting, and learning to stay calm and efficient under pressure. With CateringPor, after airside and ramp safety training, I worked on aircraft for airlines including Emirates, where every detail mattered.

In the UK, I supervised the repack department at Brand Addition and travelled to Germany to train staff, and I qualified as a workplace first aider and in the rules for shipping lithium batteries by air, road and sea. Alongside work and family, I completed a BA with First Class Honours and then an MSc with Distinction, while working as a freelance virtual assistant. Since 2023, I have volunteered with Aviva Manchester Church, in children’s ministry, media and conference support.

Today, I bring all of this into how I approach technology: I start with how the work is done, because I have done so much of it myself.
