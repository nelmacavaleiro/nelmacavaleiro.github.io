Nupcial: architecting and launching a two-sided SaaS platform

Designing, building and launching a live platform for couples and wedding vendors, from prototype to production.

Role
Founder and developer
Period
February 2026 to present
Focus
Architecture and delivery
Status
Live

Context

Nupcial is a wedding planning platform with two sides: couples who plan their wedding, and vendors who offer services to them. It is live at nupcial.app, and I own the whole lifecycle, from product concept and UX through to architecture, development and deployment.

Approach

I produced a first prototype from a single prompt in Google AI Studio at a Manchester Digital event. I then rebuilt the product entirely over approximately three months, using Claude Code for AI-assisted development and debugging, so that every part of the architecture was deliberate rather than generated.

Architecture

The client is built with React and Vite, with Zustand managing application state. Supabase provides authentication, a PostgreSQL database and Edge Functions for server-side logic. Stripe Connect handles vendor payments and the platform's commission.

Key decisions

  • Data isolation by design. Row-level security in the database means each couple can only ever read and change their own wedding data, enforced at the data layer rather than in the interface.
  • Reliable account creation. Wedding creation at sign-up is idempotent, so a repeated request cannot create duplicate records.
  • A business model that lowers the barrier to adoption. Nupcial is free for couples, with a commission on vendor transactions through Stripe Connect.

Features delivered

Guest and seating management with drag and drop, budget tracking, vendor onboarding and pipeline, a wedding album and live slideshow, and an AI assistant.

Outcome

Nupcial is publicly launched and in ongoing development. I used it to plan my own wedding, and our guests used it on the day.

Tools

React, Vite, Zustand, Supabase, Stripe Connect, Git and GitHub, Claude Code
