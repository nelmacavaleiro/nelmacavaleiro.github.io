Brand Addition: running a repack department from request to dispatch

Supervising over 400 repack and logistics projects a year, with the tracker and workflow guide I built to keep them on time and on budget.

Role
Repack Supervisor
Period
September 2018 to November 2023
Focus
Operations, data and process
Status
Completed

Context

Brand Addition repacks and ships branded products for UK and international clients. The repack department handled a high volume of projects, each with its own deadline, budget and quality requirements, across sites in Manchester and Gelsenkirchen.

My role

As Repack Supervisor, I ran the department end to end, from receiving a client request to dispatching the finished goods, delivering over 400 projects a year. I led and trained the team in the UK and Germany, supervised the production line to quality standards, and acted as the department’s first aider.

What I built

  • A scheduling, costing and KPI tracker. I designed the department’s Excel tracker (Pivot Tables, variance analysis), recording each project’s dates, hours, progress and labour, material and carriage costs, so projects stayed on deadline and on budget. I used it to report progress, costs and staff and agency hours to management.
  • The department’s first official workflow guide. Before it, new staff struggled to follow how to request and deliver a repack. The guide documented the end-to-end process, from billing references to customs documents and courier formats, and simplified onboarding.

Systems and compliance

  • Managed orders and stock in the internal ERP system, validating courier data imports and reconciling inventory to minimise dispatch errors.
  • Produced the shipping documentation, including customs declarations and commercial invoices, and was authorised to prepare shipments containing lithium batteries after CAA-approved dangerous goods training.

Outcome

A department with clear visibility of every project’s progress and cost, a documented process that new staff could follow, and over 400 projects a year delivered for clients in the UK and abroad.

Scale

Over 400 projects a year for UK and international clients, across the Manchester and Gelsenkirchen sites

Tools

ERP system, Excel (Pivot Tables, variance analysis), ETL validation

Training

CAA-approved carriage of lithium batteries by air, plus road and sea; First Aid at Work
