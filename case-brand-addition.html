Brand Addition: operational visibility and process standardisation

Coordinating up to 300 international projects a year through an ERP system, KPI reporting and standardised workflows.

Role
Repack Supervisor
Period
September 2018 to November 2023
Focus
Operations, data and process
Status
Completed

Context

A high volume of international repack and logistics projects for clients worldwide, with tight deadlines, strict quality requirements and teams working in two countries.

My role

I coordinated up to 300 international projects each year and managed a cross-site team in the United Kingdom and Germany, handling resource allocation, scheduling conflicts and communication throughout the project process.

Approach

  • Operated an internally developed ERP system daily for order processing, stock control, client management, warehouse operations and project tracking.
  • Validated courier batch data imports using ETL processes and reconciled inventory to reduce dispatch errors.
  • Built KPI dashboards in Excel using Pivot Tables and variance analysis.
  • Wrote the department's first official workflow document.

Outcome

A 45% increase in resource allocation efficiency through regularly produced reports, and standardised processes that shortened training for new staff.

Scale

Up to 300 international projects a year, with a cross-site team across the UK and Germany

Tools

ERP system, Excel (Pivot Tables, variance analysis), ETL validation
