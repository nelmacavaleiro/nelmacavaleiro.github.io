MSc research: the influence of Business Intelligence in project management

A mixed-methods study of how BI is used in project work, and what stands between the two disciplines.

Role
Researcher
Period
2026
Focus
BI and project management
Status
Completed

Context

My MSc Information Systems Management dissertation at the University of Sheffield examined how Business Intelligence is used in project work, and what stands between the two disciplines.

Method

A mixed-methods study combining 11 semi-structured interviews with project management and Business Intelligence practitioners and 33 survey responses, conducted with university ethics approval.

Themes explored

  • Familiarity with BI and the organisational context.
  • How BI tools are used in project work.
  • The effect on project performance and decision-making.
  • Challenges, barriers and the gap between BI and project management.
  • The experience of people who hold both responsibilities.

Why it matters for my work

The research grounds my view that information systems succeed when they fit how people already make decisions, which is the starting point of every solution I design.

Sample

11 interviews and 33 survey responses
